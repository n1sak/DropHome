# Demo video: a two minute shot list

Record at 1440 × 900 or larger, in Chrome, with sound on (the house makes small noises when things open and land). Use "Reset the sample house" in the menu before each take so the porch has boxes on it and there is dust to find.

Have a folder of six to ten of your own messy files ready on the desktop: a recipe, a problem set, a photo or two named `IMG_1234.jpg`, a lease or a tax form, a code file, something with "draft" in the name.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | A cluttered Downloads folder, or a wall of "Untitled (3)" files | "This is where my files live. I have no idea where anything is, and I made this mess myself." |
| 0:08 | Cut to the whole house, daytime | "Nobody remembers a folder tree. Everybody remembers a house. This is Roomy: Dropbox, but you live in it." |
| 0:16 | Click the study. Hover the closet so the doors swing, then open the desk | "Rooms are the parts of my life. Furniture means something. The desk is what I am working on now. The closet is last semester." |
| 0:28 | Open a note, show the preview, press Esc twice | "Files are things. Notes are paper, photos are polaroids, and the old ones are actually dusty." |
| 0:36 | Back to the house. Drag your folder of messy files onto the house. Boxes pile up on the porch | "Here is the chaos part. I drop a pile of files on the porch." |
| 0:44 | Click Unpack. Let the whole flight sequence play. Read the toast | "And the house unpacks them. Claude reads the names and the first lines and carries each one to the right room. The recipe goes to the kitchen. The lease goes in the safe." |
| 0:58 | Open one of the files that just moved, point at "Why here" | "Every placement comes with a reason, and one click undoes all of it." |
| 1:04 | Press `/`, type a word from one of your files, hit Enter | "When I need something, I do not search. I ask, and the house walks me there." |
| 1:14 | Living room. Pin a photo from the coffee table. Show it appear on the wall and the TV | "Pin a photo and it goes up on the wall and plays on the TV. Pin a grocery list and it is on the fridge." |
| 1:24 | Switch to night | "At night, the lights are on in the rooms I have actually been using. One look tells me where my week went." |
| 1:32 | Spring clean. Tick through the list, press Tidy, watch files fly to the attic | "And when things gather dust, spring cleaning boxes them up and takes them to the attic." |
| 1:42 | Renovate. The house turns into a blueprint. Add a floor, build an Office, drag a desk | "It is your house, so you design it. Every room follows the same grammar, so the sorting still works in a room I just invented." |
| 1:54 | Done. Back to the full house, in daylight | "Folders made us file clerks. Roomy lets you just put things where they belong." |

## Things that look good on camera

Hovering furniture opens it, so move the mouse slowly across a room before you click anything.

Open two browser windows side by side on the same server and move a file in one. It moves in the other. That is the sync story in five seconds.

Copy the mailbox drop link (open the mailbox in server mode), open it in a phone browser, and send yourself a file. The count on the mailbox goes up on the big screen while the phone is still in your hand.

The safe, the fridge and the attic trunk have the most satisfying open animations.
